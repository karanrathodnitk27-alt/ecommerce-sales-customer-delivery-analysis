# E-Commerce Sales, Customer & Delivery Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end data analysis project using **SQL, Python, and Power BI** to analyze an e-commerce business across sales, customers, products, and delivery operations.

### Project Workflow

**Data → SQL Analysis → Python Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights → Recommendations**

---

## Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

- Understanding overall sales and order performance
- Identifying high-performing product categories
- Analyzing geographic sales and order patterns
- Understanding customer growth and customer value
- Evaluating order processing and delivery performance
- Identifying states with higher late-delivery rates
- Comparing estimated and actual delivery performance
- Identifying opportunities for operational improvement

# Key Findings

Based on the completed Power BI dashboard:

- Total revenue is approximately **15.84M**.
- Total orders are approximately **99K**.
- Total items sold are approximately **113K**.
- São Paulo (SP) is the strongest state by order volume and revenue.
- Health & Beauty is among the strongest revenue-generating product categories.
- Customer activity shows substantial growth over the analysis period.
- Average delivery time is approximately **12.50 days**.
- Average processing time is approximately **3.22 days**.
- Late deliveries account for approximately **6.77%**.
- Average delivery delay is approximately **8.87 days**.
- Late-delivery performance varies across customer states.
- Most deliveries are concentrated in lower delivery-time ranges, with a smaller group of longer deliveries.

---

# Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Logistics in High-Delay States

Investigate courier performance, transportation routes, and fulfillment processes in states with higher late-delivery percentages.

### 2. Reduce Processing Time

Improve order confirmation, inventory allocation, and dispatch processes to reduce the time between order placement and shipment.

### 3. Focus on High-Value Markets

Give additional attention to strong-performing markets such as São Paulo through customer retention, marketing, and product availability strategies.

### 4. Prioritize High-Performing Categories

Use revenue performance to support inventory planning and marketing decisions for major product categories.

### 5. Improve Delivery Estimates

Use historical delivery performance to improve estimated delivery dates and reduce differences between estimated and actual delivery times.

### 6. Investigate Long Delivery Times

Analyze unusually long deliveries to identify potential causes such as seller processing, transportation, geography, or logistics performance.

### 7. Use Customer-Value Segmentation

Use differences in customer value across states and categories to support more targeted business strategies.
